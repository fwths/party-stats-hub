## Act II: The Fey Angle

### Session 10

- The party arrive safely in Zadash.
- After securing lodging at _The Blood Hound_ B&B, they search for [Lane Ignio](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-8030-81eb-f71983c24ed0) , consulting with [Carys](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-8032-a012-dd925cebe5a5) , a bartender in the Pentamarket, and two Everlight priests in the local temple.
- They learn Lane frequents The Leaky Tap and investigate there.
- At the pub, they find the proprietor, [Claudia](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-80b0-9793-ed73b3916941) , under attack by four assailants. In the ensuing fight, they kill two attackers and subdue the other two.

### Session 11

- The party converses with Claudia at The Leaky Tap. She reveals the attackers were Myriad members, and that Lane Ignio is being held at Bertha's Watering Hole by a Myriad member named Juran.
- Claudia reveals herself as a dryad using magic to conceal her true nature. She and Lane were working to disrupt the Myriad's shining dew operation, a drug produced by harvesting Fey Realm creatures.
- Important revelations from Claudia:
  - She knows Ari's mentor Elysia from both the Fey and Material planes. Elysia is currently imprisoned with Lane.
  - She has collaborated with Ranniel, who can travel between realms. Ranniel is investigating magic bleeding from the Material Plane, causing "darkening" in Fey regions.
  - Ranniel discovered starlight's purifying effect on these corruptions but is currently trapped in the Fey Realm.
  - There exists a Fey crossing north of Zadash near Rothengorm.

- The party infiltrates Bertha's Watering Hole:
  - Claudia, Ari, and Willow pose as customers in the speakeasy.
  - Echo, shapeshifted as a fly, conducts reconnaissance and hears of shining dew shipments to DWC sites.
  - Echo maps the hidden areas but triggers a trap during exploration.
  - After Echo's escape, the rest of the group retreats from the establishment.

### Session 12

- Claudia informed the party that Elysia and Lane had been relocated after the first location was discovered.
- The group investigated a Myriad safe house in Zadash where they suspected the prisoners were being held.
- **Infiltration:**
  - Echo, Qemuel, and Ari approached from the rooftop.
  - Dresana (accompanied by an invisible Willow) breached the front entrance.
  - While guards rushed upstairs, Willow slipped into the basement; Qemuel created a diversion by making his presence known.

- **Rescue:**
  - In the basement, Willow and Qemuel located and freed Elysia and Lane. Elysia swiftly overcame her guards.
  - The party reunited, defeated the remaining enemies upstairs, and made their escape.

- **Aftermath at the Leaky Tap:**
  - Elysia acknowledged her capture was due to a moment of carelessness.
  - She and Lane revealed their plans to return to the Fey Realm to halt the abductions, extending an invitation to the party.
  - Their objective: meet with Ranniel and disable the local Fey crossing.

### Session 13

- **Arrival in the Fey Realm:**
  - The party journeys to the College of Glamour, where they rejoin Lane, Elysia, and Ranniel.

- **The Fey Crossing:**
  - Intelligence suggests the Myriad is preparing for a final large-scale abduction.
  - Ranniel can seal the crossing through an hour-long ritual—the party must protect her during this time and drive back possible intruders.

- **Corellian:**
  - Twenty years ago, Lane and Corellian trained together as War Clerics of the Everlight in Vasselheim.
  - Corellian disappeared after performing a consecration ritual near Westruun using an unusual skull.
  - Ceratos remains shrouded in mystery—though some texts exist, they remain untranslated.

- **Dark Pockets in the Feywild:**
  - Ranniel has discovered unusual "pockets of darkness" containing visible starlight—a phenomenon unnatural to the Fey Realm.
  - She encountered shadow creatures similar to those the party has faced.
  - During a near-fatal encounter, her consciousness connected with Willow—potentially explaining Willow's vision.
  - These shadow pockets can be closed using magic similar to the Fey crossing ritual.

- **Dureya Wagon Company:**
  - Kel Tumal is believed dead with near certainty.
  - DWC is orchestrating a fascist uprising against the Empire, with operations in Rothengorm and Centera.

### Session 14

- The party departed from the College of Glamour and made their way to the Fey Crossing.
  - A fierce battle erupted when a significant number of Myriad agents invaded the area.
  - Though the party successfully neutralized the threat, Claudia fell in battle while protecting the ponds from the Myriad's attempt to abduct a final batch of fey creatures.
  - Ranniel survived the attack and is now completing the ritual to close the Crossing.

### Session 15

- After Ranniel closed the crossing, the party returned to the college to rest and gather supplies. They also brewed healing potions for their journey ahead.
- Ranniel shared additional information about the dark spots (intrusions) in the Fey Realm:
  - So far, she had closed 17 intrusions, with two being notably larger and more intense. During the sealing of one of these larger intrusions, she struggled, likely the moment when she unknowingly reached out to Willow mentally.
  - The cause of these intrusions remains unknown.
  - Some intrusions contained a mysterious presence, while others felt as if something was attempting to break through. All intrusions contained visible starlight, which is not naturally found in the Fey Realm.
  - Once sealed, intrusions typically do not reopen.
  - One large intrusion remains in the vicinity of the college, several days' journey on foot.

- The party decided to accompany Ranniel to this final intrusion.
  - En route, they encountered the Displaced Bog, which they needed to cross to reach their destination.
  - Navigation proved challenging as they had to hop between "islands" of land that moved rapidly across the water's surface.
  - During their crossing, moorbounders attacked the party. These creatures appeared reluctant to venture near the water.
  - Willow cast a ritual to detect magic on the bog while Echo, transformed into a fish, explored underwater. They discovered the bog contained massive snake-like creatures with protruding crystals that, upon contact with the islands, propelled them swiftly across the water.
  - Ranniel revealed that these creatures had never been officially documented; they were considered mythical until now.
  - The party christened these creatures "Bob," dubbing themselves "Mothers of Bob" in the process.
  - Upon reaching the bog's edge, the group found themselves surrounded by approximately ten moorbounders.

### Session 16

- MoB defeated the moorbounder ambush and continued toward their destination.
- They reached a section of woods where everything appeared darker, desaturated, and corrupted.
- The affected area spanned approximately one mile in diameter. The group proceeded to its center so Ranniel could begin the ritual to repair the tear.
- As the ritual commenced, shadows began swirling in a circle at a distance, and suddenly Echo vanished from sight.
- Seconds later she reappeared and reported the following:
  - She had been contacted by beings that appeared to be made of starlight. They called themselves the Heralds of Elarmeir.
  - They explained that something is threatening the "web" that maintains connections between planes. Shadows are seeping through these tears in the web, which need to be closed.
  - They requested the party (through Echo) to assist Ranniel with the ritual and then travel to a shrine in the Dunrock Mountains, where they could contact these beings again for a more detailed briefing.

- During the ritual, the party was attacked by powerful shadow beings and ultimately withstood an "attack" from the swirling shadows. Though exhausted, they all survived.
- After departing from the ritual site, the party rested and began planning their next moves. Before heading to Emon for further research, Ranniel offered to teleport them to their chosen destination—either near Rothengorm or Shadycreek Run.

## Act III: Vive la Résistance

### Session 17

- They decided the best course of action was to travel to [Shadycreek Run](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-8047-bcf9-d31311fadd11) and, from there, proceed to Site 2 and the shrine.
- Before setting off, Willow suggested taking a short detour to rest and enjoy some leisure time along the way.
- Ranniel teleported the group to Ank’Harel on the continent of Marquet. There, they explored the city: visiting local bars, market stalls, and taverns; shopping, eating, drinking, and even gambling a bit. Ari performed for the locals and then rested for the night.
- While in Ank’Harel, Willow contacted Jeremy to ask about potential contacts in Shadycreek Run. He recommended Jadi Bruk, a kobold living in the North Clover neighborhood, near Savalirwood.
- The next day, Ranniel teleported the party close to Shadycreek Run. She departed the following day for Emon to continue her research on the intrusions.
- During their journey toward the city, the group was attacked by wraiths at night but successfully fended them off.
- Upon arrival in Shadycreek Run, they noticed that some guards and townsfolk were wearing distinctive colors, some more subtle than others.
- The party rented a room at The Wrong Cushion. Before resting, Qemuel shared intelligence from Shaani about Site 2:
  - The site is lightly guarded.
  - It has five underground levels:
    - Level 1: Operations
    - Level 2: Smithing
    - Levels 3–5: Mines

  - The workers appear to be in a dazed or drugged state, rather than being mind-controlled.

### Session 18

- The next day, they made their way to the northern part of the town, searching for [Jadi Bruk](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-8002-a9e0-e00222be9d64) .
- Ari and Willow made first contact and negotiated their way inside, meeting with Jadi and his two co-workers: [Nil](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-8054-a679-d6145914a2e9) and [Insik](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-80c8-bd2f-fc5b82e62d5b) .
- Echo, outside with Dresana and Qemuel, spotted four people following and watching them from a distance.
- Jadi, Nil, and Inskik offered information in exchange for saltpeter from the town market. Ari and Willow accepted and departed with the rest.
- As they left, they discovered all four followers killed by a gas-bomb contraption. Qemuel recovered a map of Jadi’s house from one of the corpses.
- While Ari and Qemuel returned to warn the kobolds, the rest continued to the market.
  - Back at the house, Nil revealed the kobolds were responsible for the four deaths.

- After retrieving the saltpeter, the party and the kobolds gathered to exchange intelligence. Key points:
  - **Resistance Network:** A network of cells across Wildemount, Jeremy being a high-ranking figure.
  - **Rothengorm:**
    - **Contact:** Tom, a kobold in Rothengorm, a key member of the resistance.
    - **Status:** The city is under martial law, rounding up all non-human, non-elf, and non-half-elf species.

  - **Targets:** Dreven Lorgo and Minshul Arkford are high-ranking leaders of the fascist movement.

- The kobolds also provided the party with:
  - **Identification:** "Grinner" coins used to verify identity between resistance members.
  - **Explosives:** An assortment of munitions, including an experimental charge powerful enough to permanently decommission Site 2.

- The party spent the night at the kobolds’ place and started the journey to the Dunrock Mountains the next day.
- After a four-day trek facing blizzards, bandits, a griffin, and wolves, they reached the foot of the mountains.
- During the first watch, nearby (humanoid) activity suggested they could be close.

### Session 19

- The party traveled southeast along the mountain and located a path leading to a tunnel.
- After spotting eight individuals in black transporting a cart toward the east, the party tried to sneak up on them in order to steal their clothing and credentials, but they were seen and a fight broke out.
- Seven enemies were killed, and the survivor was interrogated.
- The party successfully persuaded him that the fight was a "misunderstanding" and that they were allies.
- <u>**Intelligence Gathered:**</u>
  - The group seems to belong to the Order of Penumbral Castigation.
  - The survivor identified [Eirem](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-8084-bf74-c875726a5736) (Forge Level contact) and Warden [Avurel](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-80e9-a741-f510cf2e5db5) .
  - The party obtained the Site 2 passphrase and five talismans.
  - Willow used her cult ring to inspect the body of the group’s spellcaster, [Faltok](pageId:30d23ac1-bdd8-80aa-b289-f334ae996eaa) .
    - She noticed leftover magical energy indicating his chest had been torn apart from the inside (visible only with the ring).

- **Site 2 Infiltration:** Willow, Ari, and Dresana wore the cultists' clothing and talismans to pose as an inspection team sent to review security measures.
  - Qemuel and (wild-shaped) Echo entered the site while invisible.

- Upon entering, Willow used the signet ring again and detected 15 large [Arcane Crystals](pageId:31a23ac1-bdd8-80e9-bacd-c16feded2eab) beneath the ground.
  - These crystals generate magic and are spanning five levels and appearing similar to those used on the Sevirol walls, once they managed to look at them after getting inside.

- <u>**Current Site Status:**</u>
  - **Eirem:** The party met with the overseer (instead of the warden), who manages the Forge level.
  - **(Penumbral) Warden Avurel:** He is currently unavailable, as he is meeting with the Chaplains.
  - **The Slaves:** There are roughly 350 slaves in the site, kept in a dazed state by the crystals.
  - **The Chaplains:** Responsible for long-distance communications and the maintenance of the crystals.

- Willow contacted Qemuel telepathically for an update:
  - Qemuel reported that they are safe and preparing to leave shortly.
  - He advised Willow’s group to remain visible at all times, avoid lingering too long inside, and confirmed they would meet at a designated point outside the facility.

After several days and a few encounters along the way, they reached the capital.

They headed to the Court of Colors district and rented a room at the Chromatic Grasshopper Inn, while Qemuel went to the[Library of the Cobalt Soul](pageId:31a23ac1-bdd8-808d-8ef0-f60daef2ee50) , where he spent the night.
